Privacy Policy for GhostBudget

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 • Package: com.ghostbudget.app

GhostBudget is an offline-first personal budgeting application. Your financial data belongs solely to you and stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or financial data. All budgets, spending buckets, transactions, savings goals, and categorization rules you enter are stored locally on your device using Android's local database storage (Room / SQLite) and SharedPreferences.

2. Backups, Exports & Android Auto Backup

  • Manual Exports: You may optionally export or share your budget data (such as JSON or text snapshots) using the Android system share sheet. Those files are only sent to the destination or app you explicitly choose.
  • Android Backup: Standard Android OS backup (android:allowBackup="true") may back up local app data to your personal Google account if enabled in your device settings, governed by Google's privacy policy.

3. Third-Party Services, Ads & Analytics

GhostBudget contains no advertisements, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no tracking or crash-reporting libraries that transmit user data off your device.

4. Permissions

  • Network / Internet Permission: Declared in the app manifest for optional future connectivity features; the current release operates locally on-device and does not upload your financial records to any server.

5. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete your data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling GhostBudget.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at the support email listed on the GhostBudget Google Play Store page.
