1. Privacy Policy

STS Software built GhostBudget: Offline Finance as a privacy-first, standalone budgeting utility. We believe your personal financial data belongs solely to you.

Zero Data Collection & Storage:

  • GhostBudget does not collect, track, log, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or financial details.
  • The application operates completely offline and requires zero network or internet permissions.
  • All transactions, budget limits, categories, and settings are stored locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database.
  • We do not operate servers, cloud sync, analytics, or background telemetry.

Third-Party Access:

  • No third-party SDKs, advertising frameworks, or trackers are embedded within the application.
  • Because no data is transmitted off your device, no third party has access to your financial records.

Data Backups & Control:

  • You maintain total ownership of your data. You can export manual backup snapshots or clear your local database at any time from within the application settings.
  • If you uninstall the application without exporting a backup, your local data will be deleted in accordance with Android operating system standards.
